# Feras Elghandour

### AI Research Engineer | Deep Learning & Autonomous Systems

I develop, train and evaluate machine-learning models, from neural architectures and generative models to reinforcement-learning policies. My work spans experimental research and engineering prototypes for sensing, control and reliable decisions.

I focus on how models learn: architecture design, training behavior, meaningful baselines and failure analysis.

## Approach

I pair implementation with evaluation: reproducible experiments, meaningful comparisons and clear operating assumptions. The portfolio includes independent research, coursework and engineering prototypes, with course materials, upstream work and AI assistance attributed where applicable.

Project documentation identifies the available implementation, historical training results, reproducibility instructions and evaluation limits. Some repositories provide public components of larger experiments; their checks are distinguished from reproduction of the original results.
